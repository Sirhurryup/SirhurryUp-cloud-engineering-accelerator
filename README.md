
# SirhurryUp Cloud Engineering Accelerator

**Eight Weeks. Eight Business Problems. Eight Engineering Solutions.**

An engineering portfolio developed under SirhurryUp Corporation to demonstrate practical cloud engineering, automation, troubleshooting, and solutions architecture.

**Guiding Principle:** We don't just build systems that work. We build systems whose design, security, operations, and costs can be explained and verified.

## Mission

Design, implement, test, and document solutions to realistic business problems using Linux, Python, AWS, networking, infrastructure automation, containers, and cloud architecture.

Each project emphasizes technical judgment, operational reliability, security, cost awareness, and reproducible engineering.

## Auditable Engineering Standard

Every project addresses six engineering questions proportionately to its scope:

1. **Architecture:** Why was this design selected?
2. **Security:** Who or what can access the infrastructure, and how is that access controlled?
3. **Reliability:** How are failures detected, investigated, and recovered from?
4. **Evidence:** What tests demonstrate that requirements are satisfied?
5. **FinOps:** What are the operating costs, cost drivers, and optimization opportunities?
6. **Operations:** Can another engineer understand, maintain, troubleshoot, and reproduce the solution?

## Engineering Workflow

**Design → Build → Verify → Document → Commit**

Documentation is captured throughout implementation, including architecture decisions, test results, troubleshooting evidence, and operational lessons.
